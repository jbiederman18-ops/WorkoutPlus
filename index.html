Spine Safety Principles

1. Abdominal Bracing: Tighten stomach muscles outward prior to lifting to support intra-abdominal pressure.

2. No Spinal Flexion under load: Never allow your lower back to round during deadlifts or squatting movements.

3. Glute Activation: Intentionally squeeze glutes at the top of hinges and squats to relieve lumbar pressure.

4. Listen to Pain: Muscle fatigue is acceptable; sharp or shooting sensations mean stop immediately.
