Spine safety principles

1Brace your abdominalsTighten your stomach outward before you lift. The pressure supports your spine from the inside.
2No spinal flexion under loadNever let your lower back round during a hinge, deadlift, or squat.
3Finish with your glutesSqueeze hard at the top of every hinge and squat. Strong glutes take the load off your lumbar spine.
4Listen to painMuscle fatigue is fine. Sharp or shooting sensations mean stop now.
