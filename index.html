Spine Rules

1Brace your abdominalsTighten your stomach outward before you lift. That pressure supports your spine from the inside.
2No spinal flexion under loadNever let your lower back round during a hinge, deadlift, or squat.
3Finish with your glutesSqueeze hard at the top of every hinge and squat. Strong glutes take load off your lumbar spine.
4Listen to painMuscle fatigue is fine. Sharp or shooting sensations mean stop now.
5Know which back you haveThis program assumes bending forward is what bothers you, which is the most common pattern. If your pain comes from arching back instead, some of these exercises are wrong for you. Get assessed by a physio or doctor before week 1.
